GeoRemind is a smart, location-based reminder application designed to help users complete tasks that are tied to specific physical locations. Unlike traditional to-do lists that rely on time, GeoRemind uses potential geofencing technology to trigger notifications exactly when the user enters a relevant area. whether it's buying milk near the grocery store or picking up a package when near the post office, GeoRemind ensures you never miss a task because you "forgot you were close by."
